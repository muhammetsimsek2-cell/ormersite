Araştırma Görevlisi, Dr. Recep Tayyip Gürler

 

Recep Tayyip Gürler graduated from Department of International Relations at Sakarya University in 2012. At the same year he began his study for master degree in Department of Middle Eastern Studies at the same University. He also works as Research Assistant at Sakarya University since 2012. He is still studying on Iraq at Center for Middle Eastern Studies at Sakarya University.

rtgurler@gmail.com
