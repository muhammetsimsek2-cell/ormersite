The Middle East Studies Non-Thesis Master’s Program Overview

 

The program provides students with the knowledge, skills, and contacts to pursue a career in a large range of branches of international affairs, including intergovernmental organizations, NGOs, government, diplomacy, international business, journalism, and academia. Graduates of this online master’s program are awarded a Non-Thesis Online Master’s degree accredited in Turkey by Higher Education Council (YÖK). The program curriculum has a multi-disciplinary scope. Required courses cover the core disciplines of international relations, political science, economics, international organizations, and diplomacy; a broad range of electives is available to explore other areas of international affairs of the Middle East

In addition, our education focuses on personal development, giving the students the necessary training and support to be successful in their careers after the Master’s program. The program seeks to provide a comprehensive study of the Middle East, with special emphasis on theoretical and regional issues such as security, foreign policy, diplomacy, identity, geopolitics, political geography, migration, religion, civil society, societal movements, political Islam.

The Social Science Institute emphasis on providing adequate skills for academic work and its concern with the development of in-depth knowledge of the topics of the Middle East are reflected at the graduate level with a series of advanced courses. Courses are basically designed as online seminars in which student participation is essential. For those who have received their undergraduate degrees from programs other than international relations can also apply this online master program. Thirteen courses are to be taken within a period of three semesters. Each course meets three hours per week, accounting for 3-credits.

The Middle East Studies Non-Thesis Master’s program is a one and a half-year program. Students typically take four courses every semester. The program may be taken part-time. This is particularly suitable for professionals who wish to integrate the program into their work schedule. Scheduling may be arranged according to each student’s particular constraints.

Methods

How it’s done – studying Non-Thesis Middle East Studies Online ProgramSakarya University’s advanced graduate degree program Non-Thesis Middle East Studies Online program combines e-learning with regular periods of on-site-instruction. It is thus a blended learning program, which provides both the flexibility of a distance learning program and the advantages of personalized instruction and face-to-face interaction

Program Outcomes

• Graduates will have a working knowledge of several subfields of the Middle East Studies

• Graduates will have strong research skills in the Middle East Studies

• Graduates will be able to critically analyze international events and issues relating to the Middle East.

• Graduates will be able to apply theories of international politics to the practice of international, societal and political affairs in the Middle East.

• Graduates will demonstrate effective written and online communication skills.

Saban Kardas (Assoc. Prof.) works as Associated Professor in the Department of International Relations at TOBB Economy and Technology University in Ankara. He has published scholarly articles and book chapters on Turkish domestic and foreign policies, human rights, and international security and has been an occasional contributor to Turkish and international media. Ertan Efegil (Assoc. Prof. Dr.) got his PhD from Gazi University in 1999. He prepared his master thesis on the United Nations Protection Force in former Yugoslavia. He taught at the Eastern Mediterranean University in the Department of International Relations, from 1999 to 2004; at the Cankaya University in the Department of Political Science and International Relations from 2004 to 2005; and lastly at the Beykent University in the Department of International Relations from 2005 to 2008. He has prepared several English and Turkish edited national and international books, chapters in books and essays on the matters of foreign policy making, Cyprus question, Central Asia, European Union, Turkish foreign policy and the Middle East. He is recently working on the subject of foreign policy analysis. He is a member of Harvard University Central Asia Society. He has organized several conferences and workshops on Turkish foreign policy.

Admission Requirements

You must have at least a Bachelor\’s Degree at the time you apply for a Sakarya University Online Master\’s Degree Program. Preferably this degree has been obtained in Political Sciences, Humanities, or Social Sciences, including Law and Economics. However, it isn’t necessary that the degree be in a international relations (IR)-related field. In fact, the certificate program is perfect for those who have a non-IR-related bachelor’s degree, but who are looking to add to their understanding of IR and principles in general, and the Middle East Studies concepts and practices in particular. To successfully complete the program, access to adequate electronic equipment – in particular, internet access and a computer capable of working with multimedia course materials – is mandatory.

How to apply

All application documents must be sent via e-mail (emest@sakarya.edu.tr) Your application must include the following: • Curriculum vitae (in English) • Certificate of a relevant academic degree (B.A. or higher) • Official transcripts that show the entire undergraduate and courses taken, grades received, and the grade point averages obtained. If English is not your native language, a language certificate verifying English proficiency is required. • For Turkish citizens it is required at least 60 from KPDS or ÜDS; if English is your first language, you do not need to prove it just send the first page of your passport; for non-Turkish citizens whose first language is not English it is required to be proven English language ability with TOEFL or IELTS. Please note: all documentation must be submitted with a certified translation if written in any language other than English. Selection criteria for enrollment in the program are: Middle East Studies Non-Thesis Online Master program seeks qualified candidates with strong analytical skills, dedication, and a genuine interest in the Socieal Science. For the selection process, your application is therefore evaluated within the following parameters: • Field and quality of your first university degree (transcript) (50%) • English language competency (50%) For Turkish applicants: • 50% of Undergraduate GPA Successful candidates will be informed and offered a place in the program by August 15 at the latest.
