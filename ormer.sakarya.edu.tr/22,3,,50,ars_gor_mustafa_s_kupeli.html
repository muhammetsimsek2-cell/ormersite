Kadromuz

Arş. Gör. Mustafa Ş. Küpeli

 

Mustafa Şeyhmus KÜPELİ graduated from Department of International Relations at Sakarya University in 2011. At the same year he began his study for master degree in International Relations at Sakarya University. He completed his master's degree in 2015 and began PhD education in the field of Middle East Studies. KÜPELİ took office as Research Assistant in Social Science Institute at Sakarya University in 2013. He is still studying at Political Economy in the Middle East and Iran Foreign Policy.

Master Thesis:  "The Political Economic Effects of Sanctions on Iran in the Framework Nuclear Issue"

Research Fields:

  • The Effects on Iranian Foreign Policy of the Sanctions
  • Political Economy of the Middle East
  • The Energy Politics and the International System in the Middle East
  • Iran Foreign Policy

Publications:

  •  Conference Presentations
