From Al-Qaeda to Post-Qaeda: The Evolution of ISIS

  • Articles
Jul 17, 2014
74

The Islamic State of Iraq and al-Sham (ISIS) became an important actor in Syr-ian Civil War after their expansion through Northern Syria and brutal practices in the region. ISIS also advanced in Iraq where the organisation was initially founded. In this article practices and route of ISIS are examined through the his-torical evolution of group from Invasion of Iraq (2003) to Syrian Civil War. The article also discusses different positions and practices of ISIS in fighting against Iraqi government and their struggle against Sunni Syrian rebels. This work also investigates the the reasons of ISIS’s recent advances in the region with particu-lar focus on their disownment from al-Qaeda.

Recep Tayyip Gürler & Ömer Behram Özdemir
