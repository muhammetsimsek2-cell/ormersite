Zehra Betül Güney

  • Staff
Jan 23, 2016
32


Zehra Betül Güney grew up in The Kingdom of Saudi Arabia where she completed her elementary-middle-high school education in 1995. After her graduation, she traveled to Iran to study Persian language in Imam Khumaini International University at Qazwin city. She received Persian diploma within 6 month and attended to the Department of Psychology at Faculty of Arts and Sciences in Tahran University. After her 3 semesters she attended to Arabic Literature Department at Istanbul University in 1997. She traveled to USA to study English in Harvard University, Language Institute and Boston Academy of English for TOEFL classes between 2000-2002. After her graduation from Istanbul University she worked as a translator in Noteries and as a lecturer in different private Universities and colleges between 2002-2014. In 2010, she went to earn her Master’s degree in Middle Eastern Studies, International Relations from Marmara University.  Before gaining her academic employment in Sakarya University she studied Phd for 4 years in the same department in Marmara University. She is now working as a full time lecturer in Divinity School, the Department of Arabic Literature and at the same time in the Middle East Institute at Sakarya University.

Research Interests

  • Political Islam
  • The Political History of Middle East
  • Salafism/Salafiyya
  • Egypt
  • Muslim Brotherhood
  • Jihadi/Takfiri Groups

Articles Published Journals

  • “Expulsion of Ahmad Sukkary From The Muslim Brotherhood and His Criticism of Hasan al-Banna”, Turkish Journal of Middle Eastern Studies, Volume 7, No 1, July 2015, pp: 110-131.
