Kadromuz

Prof. Dr. Kemal İnat

 

Kemal İnat graduated from Ankara University with a Bachelor of Arts in Political Sciences in 1992 and from Siegen University with a Ph.D. through his dissertation "Turkey's Middle East Policy in the Inception of 21st Century " in 2000. Editing Middle East Yearbook which is published since 2005 and Turkish Foreign Policy Yearbook that is published since 2009, Inat published articles in several national and international books as Dünya Çatışmaları and peer-reviewed journals such as Blaetter für Deutsche und international Politik, Bilgi, Turkish Journal of Middle Eastern Studies. An active professor at Sakarya University International Relations Department and Middle East Institute, Inat lectures on Issues in the Middle East, Turkey's Middle East Policy and EU-Middle East Affairs alongside the Directorship of Middle East Institute.
