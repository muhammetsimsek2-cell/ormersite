Kadromuz

Araştırma Görevlisi, Dr. A. Selcan Özdemirci Cinal

 

After graduating from the Political Science and Public Administration at Hacettepe University, she started her postgraduate education at Marmara University's Institute of Middle East Studies in Middle East Political History and International Relations. In 2011, Özdemirci-Cinal received an Arabic language education scholarship at the Language Center at the University of Jordan (II-TASOL).

After Arabic training, she started working as a research assistant at Sakarya University's Institute of Social Sciences in 2013 and took part in various field studies in Lebanon. She completed her doctoral thesis, "From the Subaltern to the Powerful: The Political Transformation of Lebanese Shiism," in 2023. Özdemirci-Cinal continues her Middle East Political History studies at Sakarya University Middle East Institute.
